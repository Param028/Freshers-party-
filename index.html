05 / QUESTIONS FROM THE CROWD

FAQ,
no filler.

Still unsure? Ask the people who have the clipboard.

Who can register for Mr & Miss Freshers?+
Second-year students from the Data Science Department only. You do not need stage experience — presence, personality and a willingness to have fun matter more.
Can groups perform?+
Absolutely. Solo acts, duos and groups up to 15 members are welcome. Add every member's name so the organizers can plan the running order and entry.
Can one person sign up for more than one performance?+
You can submit more than one act if each act is meaningfully different. Use a clear group name for each submission and let the organizers know if the same people are involved.
How will results and shortlist updates be announced?+
The organizer team will contact the phone number submitted in your form, primarily on WhatsApp. Keep an eye on your messages after registration closes.
What happens if I make a mistake in the form?+
If you spot it before submitting, fix it in the form. If you already submitted, message the organizer team with your name and roll number so they can correct the sheet entry.
